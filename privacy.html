Scope

This notice covers:

1) Public Website (www.airboxinc.com)

Access is restricted to Airbox personnel and expressly authorized parties. If you are not authorized, you have no permission to use these systems.

By accessing Internal Tools, authorized users acknowledge and agree that Airbox may collect, process, and retain information necessary for security, operations, debugging, compliance, and auditing. This may include:

  • Identifiers and metadata (IP address, user-agent, token or key IDs, timestamps, request/response metadata).

  • Security events (auth attempts, allow/deny decisions, rate-limits, anomaly detections).

Airbox may correlate this data with other internal telemetry for the purposes above. Authorized users should assume comprehensive logging is in effect.

  • Legal basis: legitimate interests in operating secure services; performance of contracts with authorized users; compliance with law.

  • Airbox may monitor, throttle, block, or report unauthorized or abusive activity.

5) Your Choices & Rights

For the public website, there is no account or tracking to manage. For Internal Tools, access is permitted only under explicit authorization and applicable agreements; rights requests should be directed through your Airbox point of contact.
